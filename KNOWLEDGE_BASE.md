# Sandbox Knowledge Base content

Exact FAQ entries added to the sandbox's "Existing knowledge base" (**AI Agents → Knowledge
Base**), referenced by step 2 of the README's setup instructions. These exist to make the
`knowledge_base` recommendation category and the `policy_violation`/`objection_handling` synthetic
transcripts (see `scripts/seed.ts`'s `SCENARIO_OVERRIDES`) land on a real, verifiable gap — the
answers genuinely exist in the account, the agent just has no knowledge-base action wired up to
reach them. Copy these verbatim into a fresh sandbox to reproduce the same demo.

---

**Q: What are your business hours?**

Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. We're closed on
weekends and on all major Indian public holidays (a full holiday calendar is published on our
website each January). Outside these hours, you can still leave a message or submit a ticket —
our team responds to after-hours requests within one business day. Enterprise-tier customers with
a signed SLA get 24/7 on-call support for critical (P1) issues, dispatched through a separate
emergency line, not through this general channel.

---

**Q: How much do your plans cost?**

We offer three tiers. Starter is $99/month for up to 500 calls, includes 1 Voice AI agent, and
standard email support. Growth is $299/month for up to 2,500 calls, includes up to 5 agents,
priority chat support, and call analytics. Enterprise is custom-priced, with unlimited agents, a
dedicated account manager, and the 24/7 SLA mentioned in our business hours policy. All plans bill
monthly with no long-term contract, and unused call minutes don't roll over. A 14-day free trial
is available on Starter and Growth — no credit card required to start.

---

**Q: Can I speak to a specialist for urgent issues?**

Yes. If your issue is urgent — service outage, billing error affecting active service, or
anything blocking your business operations — say "this is urgent" or "I need to speak to a
specialist" and you'll be connected to a live specialist immediately, bypassing the standard
callback queue. Non-urgent requests are handled via the normal contact-and-callback flow,
typically within one business day. Specialists are available during business hours only; outside
those hours, urgent requests are queued as P1 and follow the after-hours escalation path.

---

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription, and can I get a refund?**

You can cancel anytime from Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan — no phone call or approval
needed. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle; you keep full access
until then, and you won't be charged again after that. We don't offer prorated refunds for the
current billing period, but if you were charged in error (duplicate charge, billing bug), contact
support and refunds for those cases are processed within 5-7 business days. Annual plans canceled
mid-term are refunded the unused months, minus one month as an early-termination fee.

---

**Q: What services does RG Voice AI provide?**

RG Voice AI provides AI-powered voice agents that handle inbound customer calls for small and
mid-sized businesses — answering routine questions, qualifying leads, booking appointments, and
capturing contact details before handing off to a human when needed. Agents are configured per
business with a custom prompt, script, and optional integrations (calendar booking, CRM sync,
knowledge-base lookups). We also offer call analytics, transcript review, and ongoing agent
optimization as part of the Growth and Enterprise tiers. Setup typically takes under a day for a
single agent; multi-agent and custom-integration setups are scoped individually with your account
manager.

---

**Q: How do I get in touch with support outside of a phone call?**

You can reach us by email at support@rgvoiceai.example (response within one business day), or via
live chat on our website during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM IST) for Growth and
Enterprise customers. Starter customers get email support only. For account or billing questions
specifically, use the "Billing" category when submitting a ticket so it routes directly to our
billing team instead of general support, which cuts response time roughly in half. We don't
currently support SMS-based support requests.

---

**Q: How is my data handled and stored?**

Call transcripts and contact details you provide are stored encrypted at rest and are only
accessible to your account's authorized users and our support team when actively helping with a
ticket. We don't sell or share customer data with third parties. Data is retained for the length
of your active subscription plus 90 days after cancellation, after which it's permanently deleted
unless you request an earlier deletion. Enterprise customers can request a signed Data Processing
Agreement (DPA) and custom retention terms.
